# 20128 · Writing and Execution of Projects

Teaching materials for **20128 Writing and Execution of Projects**, Degree in Biology,
Universitat de les Illes Balears — academic year 2026–27.

## You are the evaluation committee

A one-hour activity in which students score a research proposal against the subject's own
evaluation sheet, calibrate their marks against two colleagues, and then score the same
proposal rewritten.

**Open it here:** https://montsecompa.github.io/20128-project-writing/

The page is a single self-contained HTML file. It needs no account, no server and no
connection once loaded: students can download it and keep working offline, and it saves
their completed committee sheet as a file they can hand in.

### The evaluation sheet it uses

| Block and section | Weight |
|---|---|
| A · Appearance of the written project | 15 % of the final grade |
| B · Title | 2.5 % |
| B · Abstract | 2.5 % |
| B · Introduction | 20 % |
| B · Objectives | 20 % |
| B · Methods + work plan + expected results | 40 % |
| B · Budget | 15 % |

Every section is scored 0–10. Block B is the weighted average of its six sections, and the
final grade is `0.15 × A + 0.85 × B`. The written project must reach 5, and it is 30 % of
the mark of the subject.

### A note on the proposal used in the activity

The bat-and-street-lighting project in both drafts is **invented**. The lighting figure, the
species and the LED replacement programme are plausible for Mallorca but they are not
citations, and should not be quoted as such.

## Price the job, then invoice it

A consultancy costing exercise for Block II, Unit 7. A client asks for a
*Posidonia oceanica* survey before a mooring application. Students write the
scope, build the fee from a rate card, meet a change of scope, and issue the
invoice with IVA and the IRPF retention.

**Open it here:** https://montsecompa.github.io/20128-project-writing/quote/

The sheet calculates as you type, keeps your work in your own browser, and
shows the model answer when you ask for it. Nothing is submitted and nothing
is handed in.
