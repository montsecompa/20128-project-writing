Step 4 · the invoice

Issue it

A Spanish invoice is a legal document, and its contents are set by Real Decreto 1619/2012. Two tax lines run in opposite directions: IVA is added, and the IRPF retention is subtracted and paid to the tax office by your client.

Any fee between about 6 000 € and 10 000 € is defensible. What is not defensible is a round number with no arithmetic behind it, or a fee that forgets the boat, the laboratory or the overhead.

  1. The scope is the price. Everything you did not exclude, you agreed to do.
  2. Reporting is the biggest block of time in most consultancy jobs, and the one costed at zero by almost every group.
  3. A change of scope is priced in writing before it is done, not argued about at the end.
  4. The number the client pays is not the number you earn. The IVA passes through you to the tax office; the retention is your income tax, paid in advance by your client.

The rate card is invented for the exercise and plausible for a small Balearic consultancy. The client, the company and the mooring application are invented too.

The tax treatment is real. IVA on professional services is 21 %. A professional invoicing a company or another professional applies an IRPF retention, normally 15 %, with a reduced rate in the first years of activity. The compulsory contents of an invoice are set by Real Decreto 1619/2012.
